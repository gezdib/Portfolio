Subcontract Log Updater

Upload the current contracts file (the MDOT tracking log) and the newest Subcontract Log PDF. The tool lines the two up by contract number, rolls the two Paid columns forward with the PDF's Paid amounts, updates the other amounts that changed, and flags new subcontractors and name mismatches for you to double check. Nothing is written without your review, and nothing is uploaded anywhere: everything runs on this page, in your browser.

1Upload the two files

The contracts file is the state you want to update. The PDF log is the newest data. Amounts are compared to the cent. A row whose contract number appears in the PDF but not in your file is proposed as a new row. A blank in the PDF never overwrites a real amount in your file: it is flagged as a warning instead. Nothing is ever deleted.

Contracts file (current state)
Excel or CSV, for example the MDOT Tracking Log (.xlsx, .xls, .csv)
Subcontract Log PDF (newest data)
The printed Subcontract Log as a text-based PDF (Print to PDF works). Scanned images will not read.
Sample data is fictional and shows a Paid roll with a new payment, one filled-in blank, one new subcontractor, and one name mismatch warning.

2Check what was read from the PDF

These are the rows extracted from the PDF log. The totals check compares the sum of the extracted rows against the totals printed on the log itself: if a row had been missed or misread, the sums would not match. You can correct any cell here before comparing, and anything you change is what gets compared.

3Paid columns and date

The columns are matched automatically: the PDF's Original, Approved and Revised amounts update the matching columns in your file, and the Paid columns roll forward as described below. The PDF's Pending, Invoiced and Retained amounts have no matching column in the tracking log and are never written.

4Review the changes

Every difference found is listed below, including the Paid columns rolling forward. Untick anything you do not want applied. Warnings never block anything, they are there for you to double check. The updated file is only produced when you press the button, and your original file is never modified.

5Download the updated file

Subcontract Log Updater. Files are processed locally in your browser and are never sent to a server.
Always spot-check the change report against the PDF before using the updated file.
